# FRIT — Refunds & Wallet Withdrawals

Last updated: October 2026.

## Subscriptions
- Unused activation codes: full refund within 7 days of payment.
- Used/expired subscriptions: non-refundable (service was delivered).
- Duplicate payment for the same period: refunded in full on report.

## Wallet
- Unspent wallet balance is YOUR money and is withdrawable anytime to your bank
  account (processing within 3 business days, transfer fees deducted).
- Debits already spent on your behalf (completed bookings, generated media,
  overage) are non-refundable.

## How to claim
Send in-app feedback with: user ID, payment reference/passcode, and reason.
Refunds go to the bank account you paid from (or wallet credit, your choice).

## Failed / unconfirmed payments
- Bank transfers with a wrong narration are matched manually within 24 hours.
- Unconfirmed crypto transactions are never credited — they auto-approve after
  1 confirmation or stay queued for manual review.
