# FRIT — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026.

## 1. What FRIT is
FRIT ("Full-Range Intelligence Terminal") is an Android AI assistant that automates
phone tasks, performs market analysis, and generates creative content. Some actions
run on your phone; reasoning, search, and billing run on our server.

## 2. Subscriptions
- Tiers: Free, Creator, Pro. Prices are quoted in Naira at the dollar rate on the
  day you subscribe; the quoted amount is locked on your order.
- Subscriptions last 30 days from activation and do not auto-renew. You resubscribe
  manually. Expired accounts fall back to Free limits — your data is kept.
- Activation codes are single-use. Do not share them; used codes cannot be reissued.

## 3. Wallet
- The wallet holds YOUR money for the AI to spend on your behalf (bookings, orders,
  services). Funding is by bank transfer or crypto to our published accounts.
- Every kobo movement is recorded in a tamper-evident ledger you can audit.
- The agent only spends within your balance and usage caps. Overage rates apply past
  tier caps and are shown in-app before debit.

## 4. Acceptable use
No fraud, no impersonation, no harassment via calls/messages, no attempts to bypass
caps, billing, or device confirmations. Financial features (MT5 trading) are
paper-first; live trading is at your own risk — FRIT is not a licensed advisor.

## 5. Availability
AI providers, networks, and phone UIs change. We do not guarantee uninterrupted
service. If a paid feature is down for over 48 hours, contact support for extension.

## 6. Termination
We may suspend accounts for abuse, non-payment, or chargeback fraud. You may stop
using FRIT anytime; wallet balances remain withdrawable per the Refunds policy.

Contact: support via in-app feedback.
